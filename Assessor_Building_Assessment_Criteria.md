# MapR — Assessor Reference: Building Construction Classification and Cost Schedule

## Source

**Document:** *Schedule of Unit Base Construction Cost for All Types of Buildings or Similar Structures*, Form 10, shown as part of an ordinance, Series of 2019 (ordinance number is not clearly legible).

**Purpose stated in the source:** The schedule of unit base construction costs is used in the classification, appraisal, and assessment of buildings and similar structures **per square meter of floor area**.

> **Scope limitation:** This document is a **building construction-type and valuation reference**, **not** a post-disaster damage-severity classification standard. It does not define criteria for *no damage, minor damage, major damage, or destroyed*, nor percentages of physical damage. MapR assessors must not derive damage labels from construction type, cost, or A/B/C grading alone.
